## Follow-up boundary

The exec cleanup defect is reproduced VM-free in intermediate red commit
`6ac103656`. The production engine transition replaced the manager inside
the authority retained by predecessor cleanup: the old root changed from
`0x9a00200000` to `0x9a00400000`. Binding that authority to the new MM also
replaces its physical publisher; deferred cleanup can then ask the new MM
to retire the old MM's arena and find no structural owner.

The repair replaces the snapshot seam with `ExecStage1Replacement`: HVF
returns its exact successor authority, which the engine adopts while the
retained predecessor keeps its image, publisher and capacity. Backends
whose engine owns rebuilt-image retirement retain that explicit disposition.
The obsolete HVF exec snapshot accessor is removed. A same-authority
successor is refused before consuming a share; a shared exec consumes only
its own share without stealing the surviving parent's capacity.

`two_live_mm_exec_successor_keeps_predecessor_table_retirement_custody`

successor's slot remains unavailable, its exact record stays mapped and its
bytes survive. Red/green logs are retained in `target/n1g6/exec-custody/`.
These are lower-layer results, not signed closure of the abort.
