## Semantics and work

`owner::x1_shared_anonymous_edits` executes protect/unmap/remap at
16/64/256/**512** pages, reads real private bytes before edits and at the moved
destination, then observes the exact CPL3 protection/absence fault. Every
ordinary case reports two owner entries/completions and zero semantic host
forwards. The partial-retirement case removes the middle page of a 16 KiB
grant while preserving the resident neighbor's bytes and subsequent protect
admission, then faults on the removed page (three entries/completions).

The VM-free budget matrix uses the **production** X86AnonymousEditor,
shared owner, real descriptor tables and grant index at 16/64/256 touched
pages by 16/512 unrelated mappings. It counts descriptor loads plus atomic
stores (10 per leaf protect/unmap; 20 per leaf remap), exact invalidation
ranges, root work against AVL height, grant committed bits and retained
mapping identity. Whole-grant retirement skips directly to grant end;
closure storage is bounded at 64 bytes. The remap plan holds both sides in
one 1,030-entry inline planning/rollback buffer; actual 512-page KVM remap
runs without a CPL0 allocator.

## Limits and remaining gates

These fixtures run one executing lane per edited MM and use local non-PCID
INVLPG. They do not prove multi-vCPU shootdown, a persistent executor pool,
physical inventory return/reuse, automatic native page-fault publication,
OCI execution or <=2x overhead. Grant custody here is the retained logical
index; real physical backing remains owned by the VM until scoped teardown.
CPL0 cross-MM selection/copy/commit and wrong-peer physical-pin rejection
remain increment 3. No Docker, full/remote acceptance or HVF runs were made.

The director reported 20 signed `el1_` regressions against the signed base
and a pipe ping-pong failure at iteration 1199 on the preceding packet.
The Mac worker's first-bad-commit/mechanism investigation is pending; no
signed compatibility or integration acceptance is claimed for this packet.
